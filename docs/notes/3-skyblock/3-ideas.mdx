# Ideas

## Wandering Trader Alternatives

* Flowing water next to a grass block and mud has a chance to convert the mud into moss when the mud is random-ticked.  
    * Converts to pale moss instead if the mud is in pale oak forest.  
* Sniffers also sniff saplings out of rooted dirts based on the biome the rooted dirt is in.
    * Consider converting the rooted dirt to dirt when a sapling is sniffed out of it.  
* Water above dripstone block in dripstone cave biome has a chance to generate pointed dripstone below when random-ticked (as opposed to needing a pointed dripstone first).  
* Lightning strike converts grass block in mushroom fields biome to mycelium.  
* Farmer also gifts wheat seed, pumpkin seed, beetroot seed, and melon seed.

## Tagging

I don't like the way the golden dandelion was implemented because repeated application alternates its effect.  
I want these to be idempotent instead and have a different way of reversing the tag (that is also idempotent).  

I don't like the way silencing mobs work in the vanilla tweaks either because it overlaps with the mob name. But the nice thing about this is that it's easy to see which one has been silenced.  
So there should be a way to see/detect these tags if we were to not use the vanilla tweak method.  

* Locking age (modify the vanilla golden dandelion, or just have an alternative independent of it)
* Silencing (not by the silencing nametag like vanilla tweak)
* Locking growth
* Locking pathfinding (not to be utilised in farms)
    * I was thinking of villager trading halls for this, but before we do this, we should profile and actually determine what's the most laggy part first.

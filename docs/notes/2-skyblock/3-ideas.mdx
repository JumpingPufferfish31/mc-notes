# Ideas

## Wandering Trader Exclusives

* Flowing water next to a grass block and mud has a chance to convert the mud into moss when the mud is random-ticked.  
    * Converts to pale moss instead if the mud is in pale oak forest.  
* Sniffers also sniff saplings out of rooted dirts based on the biome the rooted dirt is in.
    * Consider converting the rooted dirt to dirt when a sapling is sniffed out of it.  
* Water above dripstone block in dripstone cave biome has a chance to generate pointed dripstone below when random-ticked (as opposed to needing a pointed dripstone first).  
* Lightning strike converts grass block in mushroom fields biome to mycelium.  
* Farmer also gifts wheat seed, pumpkin seed, beetroot seed, and melon seed.  

## Randomness Mitigations
